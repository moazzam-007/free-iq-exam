# Editorial & Scientific Content Standards

================================================================================
1. FORBIDDEN AI MARKETING CLICHÉS (NEVER USE)
================================================================================
Do NOT generate interchangeable SaaS marketing buzzwords:
- ❌ "Unlock your potential"
- ❌ "Supercharge your productivity / brain"
- ❌ "Transform your workflow / cognition"
- ❌ "Seamless experience"
- ❌ "Powerful, next-generation AI platform"
- ❌ "Revolutionary scientific breakthrough"
- ❌ "Designed for everyone"

================================================================================
2. MANDATORY CLINICAL & SCIENTIFIC VOCABULARY
================================================================================
Always use precise, evidence-backed psychometric and clinical terms:
- ✅ **Psychometrics**: Cattell-Horn-Carroll ($CHC$) theory, Fluid Intelligence ($G_f$), Crystallized Knowledge ($G_c$), Visual Processing ($G_v$), Quantitative Reasoning ($G_q$), Processing Speed ($G_s$).
- ✅ **Validated Scales**: WHO ASRS v1.1 (ADHD), PHQ-9 (Depression), GAD-7 (Anxiety), AQ-10 (Autism), Ishihara 38 Plates (Color Blindness), Snellen Optotype (Acuity).
- ✅ **Telemetry & Hardware**: Raw latency ($\text{ms}$), millisecond precision, hardware polling rate, sub-pixel tracking.

================================================================================
3. STRICT CITATION & NON-DIAGNOSTIC DISCLOSURE
================================================================================
- Every clinical screener card must feature its authoritative protocol citation.
- Always include the mandatory non-diagnostic disclaimer:
  *"Informational screening tools only. Not a clinical psychiatric diagnosis. Consult a licensed healthcare professional for clinical evaluation."*
