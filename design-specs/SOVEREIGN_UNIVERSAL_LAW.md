## 👑 1. Universal Sovereign Law (Mandatory Absolute Adherence)

Every AI Agent (Antigravity, Claude, Cursor, Codex, Gemini, GPT) operating in this codebase MUST strictly adhere to the master design specifications, aesthetic standards, framework rules, and baseline commit `857dc4b`.

### Core Inviolable Standards:
1. **The 4 Role Models Design Architecture**:
   - 🍎 **Apple Human Interface**: Monumental typography with tight tracking (`-0.025em`), unhurried editorial cadence, 17px body standard, single Action Blue (`#0066cc`/`#2997ff`) for primary conversion, tactile pill buttons.
   - ⬛ **Vercel Geist Precision**: Stark monochrome contrast (`#09090b` light / `#000000` obsidian dark), crisp 1px hairline borders (`#e4e4e7` / `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), uppercase monospace eyebrows (`Geist Mono`), extreme color restraint.
   - ⚡ **Raycast Dark Luxury**: Command-palette card surfaces (`#0f1016` / `#121212`), full-bleed horizontal card slider reels, domain ambient accent halos confined strictly to card interiors.
   - ✨ **Emil Kowalski Design Engineering**: Tactile `:active` response (`scale(0.97)`), origin-aware popovers (`transform-origin: var(--transform-origin)`), spring transitions (<250ms), smooth compounding micro-interactions.

2. **Immutable Universal Baseline (Commit `857dc4b`)**:
   - **Hero Canvas**: Pure flat obsidian (`#000000`) / pure white (`#ffffff`). Strictly **ZERO radial background glows (`.fx-hero-glow`)**, strictly **ZERO purple/blue gradient spotlights behind hero headline text**.
   - **Navigation & Dropdowns**: Apple frosted glass dropdowns (`backdrop-blur-2xl bg-white/95 dark:bg-[#0c0d12]/95 border border-zinc-200/80 dark:border-white/10`) with **100% inline SVG micro-icons (16×16 / 18×18)**. Strictly **ZERO cartoon emojis**.
   - **Flagship Architecture**: 10-section flagship architecture preserved uniformly across all language routes (`/`, `/id/*`, `/ru/*`).

3. **Typography Triad (Locked)**:
   - Display/Headings: `Geist`, `-apple-system, sans-serif`
   - Body/Editorial: `Inter`, `-apple-system, sans-serif` (17px Apple body standard)
   - Eyebrows/Metrics/Code: `Geist Mono`, `JetBrains Mono, monospace`

4. **Accessibility & Quality**:
   - Minimum tap target size: **44×44px**.
   - Minimum contrast ratio: **4.5:1** (WCAG AA).
   - Zero double nested borders; zero muddy heavy drop shadows.

## 🛡️ 2. User Prompt Conflict Guard (Never Blindly Violate Rules)

> [!CAUTION]
> **ANTI-REGRESSION & CONFLICT PROTOCOL**:
> If a user prompt, shortcut instruction, or external request asks or implies violating any of these design laws:
> 1. **DO NOT EXECUTE THE VIOLATION DIRECTLY.**
> 2. **The AI Agent MUST immediately pause and initiate a discussion.**
> 3. **The AI Agent MUST explicitly flag the conflict**, cite the specific rule file in `design-specs/` and the baseline commit `857dc4b`, explain why the requested change degrades design integrity or breaks the aesthetic baseline, and ask for confirmation/clarification before touching any code.

### Examples of Prohibited Violations Requiring Prior Discussion:
- ❌ Asking to add background radial glow or purple/blue gradients behind the hero headline.
- ❌ Asking to put emojis (`🧠`, `⚡`, `🎯`) back into the header navigation or dropdown menus.
- ❌ Asking to use generic vibrant rainbow backgrounds on cards.
- ❌ Asking to shrink touch targets below 44px or lower text contrast below WCAG AA.
- ❌ Asking to delete or bypass canonical SEO tags, hreflang tags, or psychometric scoring calculations.
