### 🔍 Competitor Research & Value-Add Challenge:
1. **Analyze Existing Competitors:**
   - Investigate existing online ADHD screeners (e.g., ADDitude Magazine, PsychCentral, BetterHelp, Cerebral, Mind-Diagnostics, ClarityClinic).
   - **Identify their weaknesses & dark patterns:** Almost all current commercial screeners lure users through 18–25 questions only to hit them with an aggressive email paywall or monthly therapy subscription paywall before revealing their score. Most existing tests use dated 2005 interfaces, lack sub-score breakdowns, and fail to differentiate between inattentive and hyperactive presentations.

2. **How You Should Outclass Competitors (Deliver 10x Extra Value):**
   - You have **complete creative and architectural freedom** to decide how to design the test flow, evaluation engine, and reporting.
   - Think like a clinical psychometrician and lead UI/UX product designer:
     - **100% Free & Transparent:** Instant interactive results with zero signup, zero email gates, zero sales pitches, and zero dark patterns.
     - **Scientific Precision:** Utilize the validated **WHO ASRS v1.1** (Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale) 6-question Part A screener (the clinical threshold standard) and the comprehensive 18-question full symptom inventory.
     - **Dual Presentation Breakdown:** Calculate distinct, actionable sub-scores for:
       - **Inattentive Presentation:** Executive dysfunction, working memory lapses, distractibility, disorganization.
       - **Hyperactive-Impulsive Presentation:** Motor restlessness, verbal impulsivity, impatience, inner tension.
     - **Interactive Cognitive Hybrid (Optional / Extra Value):** Consider offering a complementary quick interactive micro-task (like a 30-second Go/No-Go impulse reaction challenge) alongside the questionnaire to provide both subjective and objective behavioral insights.
     - **Visual Radar / Symptom Matrix & Exportable Report:** Clean visual charts illustrating symptom severity (Low, Moderate, High Likelihood) with an exportable/printable summary sheet formatted to help users discuss their results with a psychiatrist or healthcare provider.
