### 🎨 DESIGN ROLE MODELS & AESTHETIC BENCHMARKS TO EVALUATE AGAINST

1. **Obsidian & Minimalist Luxury (Apple / Vercel Aesthetic)**:
   - Primary Backgrounds: True Deep Obsidian (`#000000` / `#09090b` / `#0c0a09`) with subtle 1px border separation (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
   - High-Contrast Light Mode: Crisp porcelain white (`#ffffff` / `#fafafa`) with deep charcoal typography (`#09090b`).
   - Accent System: Curated semantic glows (Emerald `#10b981`, Amber `#f59e0b`, Cyan `#06b6d4`, Indigo `#6366f1`, Rose `#f43f5e`) — zero generic saturated primaries.

2. **Typography & Spatial Hierarchy**:
   - Modern Font Stack: Outfit / Inter (Headings & UI) + JetBrains Mono (Technical data, timestamps, milliseconds, scorecards).
   - Line Length & Scannability: Max text container widths (65–75ch) for long-form guides; generous line heights (`1.6` - `1.75`).
   - Visual Hierarchy: Clear progression from massive punchy `<h1>` titles down to subtle muted `[10px]` metadata tags.

3. **60 FPS Interactive Canvas & SVG Standards**:
   - All visualizers (Audio spectrums, Aim trainer canvases, Concentric circle rings, Macro doughnut charts, Life-in-weeks grids, Radar spider charts) must feature High-DPI (`devicePixelRatio`) sharpness, zero canvas blur, and smooth requestAnimationFrame cleanup.
   - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` media queries across all continuous physics/sine drifts.

4. **Touch Ergonomics & Mobile Experience (320px - 768px)**:
   - All interactive touch targets (Likert buttons, sliders, pills, radio selectors) must maintain a minimum 44px–48px hit area.
   - Zero horizontal overflow (`overflow-x: hidden`), zero layout clipping, and zero cramped multi-column squishing on small screens.
