### 🔬 CLINICAL FOUNDATION & PSYCHOMETRIC ENGINE: THE PHQ-9 STANDARD
Do not use informal or unvalidated pop-psychology quizzes. The tool must implement the clinical gold-standard **Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9)**, developed by Drs. Robert L. Spitzer, Janet B.W. Williams, and Kurt Kroenke:

#### The 9 Clinical Diagnostic Criteria (Assessing the past 2 weeks):
1. **Anhedonia:** Little interest or pleasure in doing things.
2. **Depressed Mood:** Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless.
3. **Sleep Disturbance:** Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much.
4. **Fatigue:** Feeling tired or having little energy.
5. **Appetite Change:** Poor appetite or overeating.
6. **Low Self-Worth / Guilt:** Feeling bad about yourself — or that you are a failure or have let yourself or your family down.
7. **Concentration Impairment:** Trouble concentrating on things, such as reading or watching television.
8. **Psychomotor Agitation / Retardation:** Moving or speaking so slowly that other people could have noticed, or being so restless that you have been moving around more than usual.
9. **Self-Harm / Hopelessness Ideation:** Thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself in some way.

#### Scoring Matrix & Diagnostic Brackets (Score 0 – 27):
Each question offers 4 standardized options:
- **0:** Not at all
- **1:** Several days
- **2:** More than half the days
- **3:** Nearly every day

**Clinical Severity Stratification:**
- **0 – 4: Minimal or None:** Depressive symptoms are absent or sub-clinical. Focus on routine wellness and stress management.
- **5 – 9: Mild Depression:** Mild symptoms present. Watchful waiting, sleep optimization, exercise, and social support.
- **10 – 14: Moderate Depression:** Clinical symptoms warranting professional consultation, lifestyle adjustments, and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT).
- **15 – 19: Moderately Severe Depression:** Significant functional impairment. Active medical/psychotherapeutic treatment strongly indicated.
- **20 – 27: Severe Depression:** Immediate psychiatric evaluation, psychopharmacology, and comprehensive clinical intervention recommended.
