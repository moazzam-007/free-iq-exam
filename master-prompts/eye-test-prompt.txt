### 🚀 WHY COMPETITORS FAIL & YOUR 10X VALUE-ADD CHALLENGE
Competitors fail because:
1. **No Screen Calibration:** A letter on a 13-inch laptop is physically smaller than on a 27-inch 4K monitor. Without millimeter-level screen calibration, online Snellen tests are scientifically meaningless.
2. **Ugly & Clunky UI:** Outdated tables, blurry low-res JPEG charts, distracting ads, and zero responsive design.
3. **Aggressive Lead-Gen:** Forcing users to enter their email, full name, and phone number before seeing basic results.
4. **Single-Test Limitation:** They only show one basic chart and ignore astigmatism, contrast, or color balance.

**Your 10x Value-Add Superpowers:**
1. **Interactive Screen Calibration (Physical Object Scaling):** Allow users to calibrate their screen size using a standard credit card / ID card slider or on-screen slider. This calculates exact Pixels-Per-Millimeter (PPM) so optotypes subtend precisely 5 arcminutes at a given testing distance (e.g., arm's length ~60cm or 1-2 meters).
2. **Comprehensive 4-in-1 Vision Suite:**
   - **Module 1: Visual Acuity (Sharpness):** Interactive Tumbling 'E' or Landolt 'C' chart (standardized for all languages/ages; user clicks arrow keys or on-screen direction pads). Tests 20/100 down to 20/20 and 20/15.
   - **Module 2: Astigmatism Dial (Fan / Sunburst Chart):** High-precision SVG radiating lines chart. Measures corneal curvature irregularities (identifies if specific line axes appear darker, blurrier, or doubled).
   - **Module 3: Duochrome Test (Red-Green Refraction Balance):** Crisp split red/green background test to detect subtle over-minus or over-plus spherical correction (nearsightedness vs farsightedness tendency).
   - **Module 4: Contrast Sensitivity:** Decreasing Weber contrast optotypes to test low-light vision and visual clarity.
3. **Single Eye Testing Workflow (Left Eye / Right Eye / Both Eyes):** Clear, gentle prompts guiding the user to gently cover one eye at a time without pressing on the eyeball.
4. **Authoritative Clinical Result Report:** An instant, elegant visual summary card showing Acuity (20/20, 20/25, etc.), Astigmatism tendency (None / Mild / Pronounced with affected axis), and recommendations. Zero paywalls, zero email requirements, 1-click printable / downloadable report.
5. **Trusted Medical Context & Disclaimers:** Professional medical disclaimer clarifying that this is a visual acuity screener, not a substitute for a comprehensive dilated clinical exam by an optometrist or ophthalmologist.
