1. **Analyze Existing Competitors:**

   - **Identify their weaknesses & limitations:** Almost all current commercial tests are aggressive lead-generation traps designed to capture email/phone numbers before locking the results behind paywalls or sales calls. YouTube videos lack interactivity, calibration, and left/right ear differentiation.

2. **How You Should Outclass Competitors (Deliver 10x Extra Value):**
   - You have **complete creative and architectural freedom** to design the user experience and test flows.
   - Think like a senior audio-software engineer and medical product designer:
     - **100% Free & Transparent:** Instant interactive results with zero signup, zero email gates, and zero ads interrupting the tone.
     - **Dual Test Capabilities:** 
       - **Mode A: Viral Ear Age / Frequency Sweep:** High-frequency tone sweep (from 8 kHz to 20 kHz) detecting the user's upper audible threshold and calculating their biological "Ear Age" (e.g., teenagers hearing 17–19 kHz vs older adults).
       - **Mode B: Pure-Tone Audiometry Screening:** Calibrated frequency checks (250 Hz, 500 Hz, 1000 Hz, 2000 Hz, 4000 Hz, 8000 Hz) testing Left vs. Right ear sensitivity to map a real visual Audiogram chart.
     - **Initial Volume Calibration Step:** A safe, comfortable setup tone before starting to prevent ear fatigue or sudden loud blasts.
     - **Pure Web Audio API Synthesis:** Zero external audio files (all pure sine wave tones synthesized dynamically with smooth gain ramps to prevent harsh speaker clicking).
     - **Visual Interactive Audiogram & Ear Age Certificate:** Visual chart plotting hearing thresholds across Normal, Mild, Moderate, and Severe categories, with an exportable/shareable result badge.
