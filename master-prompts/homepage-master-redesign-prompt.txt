2. MANDATORY DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETIC LAWS (`DESIGN_SYSTEM_RULES.md`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You must strictly follow the design principles documented in `DESIGN_SYSTEM_RULES.md`. Specifically:

### A. Strict Single-Accent Brand Color Law (NO CARTOON RAINBOWS)
- **Monochrome Foundation (95% of the UI)**:
  * Light Mode: Pure White (`#ffffff`) canvas with subtle Apple Parchment (`#f5f5f7`) section rhythms and crisp 1px zinc borders (`#e4e4e7` / `border-zinc-200`).
  * Dark Mode: Pure Obsidian Black (`#000000` / `#060709`) canvas with dark glass surfaces (`#0f1016`) and 1px glass borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Single Universal Action Accent (1-Color Rule)**:
  * Light Mode: **Royal Action Blue (`#0066cc` / `#2563eb`)**
  * Dark Mode: **Electric Sky Blue (`#2997ff` / `#3b82f6`)**
  * *Every clickable CTA, primary button, active tab indicator, and interactive link across EVERY section must use this single Action Blue. Do NOT assign different rainbow colors (no green/red/purple/yellow buttons) per section.*
- **Unified Neutral Badges**:
  * All badge pills across all categories must use identical clean styling (`bg-zinc-100 text-zinc-700 dark:bg-zinc-800 dark:text-zinc-300 font-mono text-xs border border-zinc-200 dark:border-zinc-700`).

### B. Typography Standards (STRICT: NO TINY / CRAMPED TEXT)
- **Display H1**: `48px – 64px` (`text-4xl sm:text-5xl md:text-6xl`), `font-extrabold`, tight tracking (`tracking-tight`), line height `1.08`.
- **Section H2**: `32px – 44px` (`text-3xl sm:text-4xl md:text-5xl`), `font-bold`, tight tracking.
- **Card Titles H3**: `20px – 24px` (`text-xl sm:text-2xl`), `font-semibold`.
- **Body Copy**: `16px – 18px` (`text-base sm:text-lg`), relaxed leading (`leading-relaxed`), comfortable reading cadence.
- **Eyebrows / Badges**: `13px – 14px` (`text-xs sm:text-sm`), `font-mono uppercase tracking-wider`.
- **CRITICAL**: *Never allow any text to drop below 13px anywhere on the page.*

### C. Layout & Interaction Physics
- **No Clunky Double Borders**: Cards must have single-level clean anatomy — never nest an inner bordered box inside an outer bordered card.
- **Pill Button Physics**: Full pill primary CTAs (`rounded-full` / `100px`) with tactile spring press feedback (`hover:scale-[1.01] active:scale-[0.98]`).
- **Full-Width Viewport Bleeds**: For any horizontal carousel or showcase reel, keep the section header container-aligned (`max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6`), but allow the interactive card track to bleed 100% full-width (`100vw`) with smooth edge gradient mask fades.
- **Mobile First**: All touch targets must be `min-h-[44px]` with fluid swipe, drag, and tap interactions.
