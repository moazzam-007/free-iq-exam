### STRICT ARCHITECTURAL & DESIGN SYSTEM RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE):

1. **Option 1 Typography Triad (Strictly Enforced)**:
   - **Headings & H1/H2**: `font-family: 'Geist', -apple-system, sans-serif;` with tight tracking (`-0.025em` on H1, `-0.02em` on H2s).
   - **Body Standard**: `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, sans-serif;` locked to Apple 17px standard (`text-[17px] sm:text-lg`, 1.62 leading).
   - **Badges, Likert Scale Values, Severity Tiers & Telemetry**: `font-family: 'Geist Mono', 'JetBrains Mono', monospace;` with a strict >= 13px floor (`text-[13px] sm:text-sm`).

2. **1-Color Action Blue + Monochrome Duet**:
   - **Action Blue**: `#0066cc` (Light) / `#2997ff` (Dark) with `#0071e3` / `#3b82f6` hover states.
   - **Surfaces**: `#ffffff` / `#f5f5f7` (Light) vs `#000000` / `#090a0f` / `#0f1016` (Dark).
   - **Hairlines**: `1px solid var(--fx-line)` (`#e4e4e7` Light, `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` Dark).
   - **FORBIDDEN**: Remove ALL violet/purple/indigo gradients (e.g., `border-violet-200 bg-violet-50 text-violet-700`, `bg-purple-50`). Replace with clean monochrome badge tokens and Action Blue accents.
   - **Clinical Severity Highlights**: Semantic Emerald (`#10b981` Minimal) / Blue (`#0066cc` Mild) / Amber (`#f59e0b` Moderate) / Rose (`#f43f5e` Severe) without neon saturation.

3. **Candidate 2 Luxury Ambient Radial Glow**:
   - Apply subtle optical depth behind the screener hero sections:
     `--fx-hero-glow-light: radial-gradient(circle at 50% 10%, rgba(0, 102, 204, 0.10) 0%, rgba(0, 102, 204, 0.03) 45%, transparent 70%);`
     `--fx-hero-glow-dark: radial-gradient(circle at 50% 10%, rgba(41, 151, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(0, 102, 204, 0.04) 45%, transparent 70%);`
   - Guarded with `display: none` under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

4. **Emil Kowalski Tactile Physics**:
   - Likert response buttons (0 to 3 / 1 to 4 / Agree vs Disagree), Go/No-Go task buttons, and navigation controls: `active:scale(0.97)` with `160ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`.
   - Option hover: `translateY(-2px)` with 1px border brightening and ambient halo.
   - All interactive touch targets >= 44px.
