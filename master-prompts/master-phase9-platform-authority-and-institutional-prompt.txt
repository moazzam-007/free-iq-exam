   - **Surfaces**: `#ffffff` / `#f5f5f7` (Light) vs `#000000` / `#090a0f` / `#0f1016` (Dark).
   - **Hairlines**: `1px solid var(--fx-line)` (`#e4e4e7` Light, `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` Dark).
   - **FORBIDDEN**: Remove ALL rainbow gradient text (`bg-clip-text text-transparent bg-gradient-to-r`), purple/indigo buttons, and AI-slop decorative borders.
   - **Academic & Whitepaper Styling**:
     - Formula containers in clean monochrome boxes with 1px hairlines and `Geist Mono` notation.
     - Psychometric citation callouts in subtle slate/zinc boxes with Action Blue link hovers.
     - Legal & Policy sections with clear numbering and high-contrast typography.

3. **Candidate 2 Luxury Ambient Radial Glow**:
   - Scoped optical depth behind whitepaper hero, profile scorecard, leaderboard header, and 404 state:
