- Russian: `src/pages/ru/games/*.astro`
- Indonesian: `src/pages/id/permainan/*.astro`

Audit the core generation and answer-evaluation logic for all 10 games:

1. **3D Mental Rotation (`rotation.astro` / `rotasi-mental.astro`)**:
   - **Cube Generation**: Inspect `createTrial()` — verify `VALID_ROTATIONS` permutation arrays.
   - **Same vs. Different Logic**: Verify how `isSame` is set and how `alteredColor` / `modifiedFace` is applied.
   - **Evaluation Match**: Check `handleAnswer(userSame)`. Verify `var isCorrect = userSame === currentTrialData.isSame`. Ensure selecting "Same" when cubes are identical is NEVER scored as false.
   - **Contrast & Color Palette**: Verify all cube face colors (`PALETTES`) are distinct and visible in dark mode.

2. **Stroop Clash (`stroop-clash.astro`)**:
   - **Word vs. Ink Match**: Inspect congruent vs. incongruent trial generation (`wordColor` vs `inkColor`).
   - **Evaluation**: Verify button match checks (`userChoice === (wordColor === inkColor)` or `userChoice === inkColor`). Ensure no inverted boolean conditions.

3. **Symbol Match (`symbol-match.astro` / `simbol-cocok.astro`)**:
   - **Symbol Grid & Target**: Verify target symbol against candidate symbol matrix.
   - **Evaluation**: Verify `handleClick()` accurately checks if selected coordinate matches `targetSymbolIndex`.

4. **Mental Math Sprint (`math-sprint.astro`)**:
   - **Equation Generator**: Verify arithmetic equation generation ($+, -, \times, \div$).
   - **Answer Calculation**: Verify computed `currentProblem.ans` matches mathematical ground truth.
   - **Options & Feedback**: Verify correct answer is present in options and clicking it awards points.

5. **Categorical Syllogism (`syllogism.astro` / `silogisme.astro`)**:
   - **Premise 1 + Premise 2 $\to$ Conclusion**: Verify all 15 formal syllogistic problem sets.
   - **Truth Value**: Check ground-truth logic keys (Valid vs. Invalid / True vs. False deduction).

6. **Eriksen Flanker Task (`flanker-test.astro` / `tes-flanker.astro`)**:
   - **Direction Target**: Central arrow ($\leftarrow / \rightarrow$) surrounded by congruent (`<<<<<`) or incongruent (`<<><<`) flankers.
   - **Evaluation**: Verify arrow keypress / button click matches the CENTRAL arrow direction, ignoring flankers.

7. **Dual N-Back (`n-back.astro`)**:
   - **Position & Audio History**: Verify N-step circular buffer history (`history[current - N]`).
   - **Match Evaluation**: Verify spatial match (`posMatch`) and letter/audio match (`soundMatch`) logic.

8. **Memory Matrix (`memory-matrix.astro`)**:
   - **Grid Pattern**: Verify randomized active tile coordinates array.
   - **User Selection**: Verify clicked tiles array matches the flashed active tiles without off-by-one errors.

9. **Digit Span (`digit-span.astro`)**:
   - **Sequence Matching**: Forward span (exact sequence order) and Backward span (exact reverse order `sequence.slice().reverse()`).

10. **Sequence Rush (`sequence-rush.astro`)**:
    - **Ascending Order**: Verify click order validation array (`1, 2, 3...` or `A, B, C...`).
