### 🚀 WHY COMPETITORS FAIL & YOUR 10X VALUE-ADD CHALLENGE
Competitors fail because:
1. **No Echo Playback:** Users can't actually hear what they sound like to other people on Zoom, Discord, Teams, or Meet.
2. **Crude Volume Bars:** They show an uncalibrated 0-100% bar instead of standard logarithmic decibels (dBFS) or RMS volume.
3. **No Noise Floor Diagnostics:** They don't tell the user if background fan noise, hum, or room echo is ruining their call.
4. **Device Locking:** They don't allow easily toggling between Bluetooth AirPods, USB headset, and built-in laptop microphones.

**Your 10x Value-Add Superpowers:**
1. **Multi-Device Input Selector:** Use `navigator.mediaDevices.enumerateDevices()` to populate a clean dropdown of all connected audio input devices (Headset, USB Mic, Webcam mic, Internal mic) so users can test and compare specific devices.
2. **Real-Time Dual Audio Visualizer (Web Audio API):**
   - **Oscilloscope Waveform:** Silky smooth live waveform display showing speech amplitude and clarity.
   - **Frequency Spectrum Visualizer:** Real-time 32/64-bar frequency visualizer separating Lows (bass), Mids (vocal clarity), and Highs (treble/hiss).
3. **Studio-Grade VU & Decibel Meter (dBFS):**
   - True RMS & Peak decibel meter (-60 dB to 0 dB) with industry color zoning: Green (Good Signal: -24 dB to -6 dB), Yellow (Hot: -6 dB to -2 dB), and Red (Clipping / Distortion: 0 dB).
4. **Killer Feature — 5-Second Playback Echo Test ("Hear What Others Hear"):**
   - 1-click test: Prompts the user to speak for 5 seconds (with circular countdown progress), then immediately loops back the recorded audio through their speakers/headphones.
   - Instant visual diagnostic rating: `Voice Clarity: Crystal Clear / Good / Too Quiet / Distorted`.
5. **Background Noise Floor & Static Detector:**
   - Detects ambient room noise when the user is silent. Diagnoses whether background hum (AC, fan, PC coil whine) is acceptable or problematic for professional calls.
6. **Live Vocal Pitch & Frequency Detector (Hz):**
   - Computes fundamental pitch (e.g., 110 Hz - 220 Hz human speech range) and sample rate (e.g., 44.1 kHz / 48 kHz).
7. **100% Privacy Guarantee:** Prominent privacy badge: Audio processing is 100% client-side in RAM. Zero audio recordings are stored, saved, or uploaded to any server.
