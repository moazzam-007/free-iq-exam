### 📦 FILE 1 SPECIFICATIONS: `src/scripts/tools/depression-screener.js`
This file must be a modular, self-executing Vanilla JavaScript assessment engine:
1. **Clinical Instrument: Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9):**
   - Developed by Drs. Robert L. Spitzer, Janet B.W. Williams, and Kurt Kroenke.
   - Evaluates symptoms over the **past 2 weeks**:
     1. Little interest or pleasure in doing things (Anhedonia)
     2. Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless (Depressed Mood)
     3. Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much (Sleep Disturbance)
     4. Feeling tired or having little energy (Fatigue / Low Energy)
     5. Poor appetite or overeating (Appetite Dysregulation)
     6. Feeling bad about yourself — or that you are a failure or have let yourself or family down (Guilt / Low Self-Worth)
     7. Trouble concentrating on things, such as reading or watching television (Cognitive / Focus Impairment)
     8. Moving or speaking so slowly that other people have noticed, or being so restless/fidgety (Psychomotor Agitation / Retardation)
     9. Thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself in some way (Self-Harm / Suicidal Ideation)
   - Supplementary Item 10: Functional impairment assessment ("If you checked off any problems, how difficult have these problems made it for you to do your work, take care of things at home, or get along with other people?").
2. **Standard 4-Point Likert Scoring (Score 0 to 27):**
   - `0`: Not at all
   - `1`: Several days
   - `2`: More than half the days
   - `3`: Nearly every day
3. **Clinical Severity Stratification:**
   - **0 – 4: Minimal or None** (Symptoms absent or sub-clinical. Focus on routine wellness and stress management).
   - **5 – 9: Mild Depression** (Mild symptoms. Watchful waiting, sleep optimization, exercise, and social support).
   - **10 – 14: Moderate Depression** (Clinical threshold. Professional evaluation, lifestyle adjustments, CBT recommended).
   - **15 – 19: Moderately Severe Depression** (Significant functional impairment. Active medical/psychotherapeutic treatment strongly indicated).
   - **20 – 27: Severe Depression** (Severe clinical crisis. Immediate psychiatric evaluation, psychotherapy, and medical consultation recommended).
4. **🛡️ CRITICAL SAFETY & CRISIS ESCALATION PROTOCOL:**
   - **Question 9 Safety Alert:** If the user scores $\ge 1$ on Question 9 (thoughts of self-harm or hurting oneself), the results screen MUST display a prominent, empathetic, non-judgmental **Emergency Crisis Safety Banner** at the very top:
     - 🇺🇸 **US & Canada:** Call or Text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline - 24/7, Free, Confidential)
     - 🇬🇧 **UK:** Call **111** (NHS Mental Health Services) or **116 123** (Samaritans)
     - 🇦🇺 **Australia:** Call **13 11 14** (Lifeline)
     - 🌐 **International:** Befrienders Worldwide (`befrienders.org`) & Find A Helpline (`findahelpline.com`)
   - Prominent Medical Disclaimer: "This tool is a self-assessment screener based on the clinical PHQ-9 criteria. It is not a medical diagnosis. If you are in immediate distress, please reach out to professional crisis resources."
5. **Interactive UI Features:**
