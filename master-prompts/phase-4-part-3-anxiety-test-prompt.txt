- **Gold Standard GAD-7 Scale (Spitzer et al., 2006)**:
  * 7 Standard Clinical Items assessing symptom frequency over the past 2 weeks:
    1. Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge
    2. Not being able to stop or control worrying
    3. Worrying too much about different things
    4. Trouble relaxing
    5. Being so restless that it is hard to sit still
    6. Becoming easily annoyed or irritable
    7. Feeling afraid, as if something awful might happen
  * Standard 4-point response scale:
    - 0: Not at all
    - 1: Several days
    - 2: More than half the days
    - 3: Nearly every day
- **Dual-Domain Subscale Analytics**:
  * **Cognitive / Affective Worry Domain** (Items 1, 2, 3, 7): Measures intrusive thoughts, catastrophic ideation, and worry loops.
  * **Somatic / Autonomic Arousal Domain** (Items 4, 5, 6): Measures physical tension, psychomotor agitation, and irritability.
- **Clinical Severity Stratification**:
  * **0 - 4: Minimal Anxiety** (Normal healthy baseline fluctuation).
  * **5 - 9: Mild Anxiety** (Sub-clinical / watchful waiting; lifestyle & stress management).
  * **10 - 14: Moderate Anxiety** (Clinically significant threshold; clinical evaluation recommended).
  * **15 - 21: Severe Anxiety** (High clinical acuity; active psychotherapeutic / psychiatric intervention warranted).
