### 🔬 SCIENTIFIC EQUATIONS & CAUTION GUARDRAILS (RIGOROUS RESEARCH)
Your calculations must adhere to peer-reviewed nutritional science standards:

1. **Dual BMR Engine:**
   - **Mifflin-St Jeor Formula (1990 - Gold Standard for General Population):**
     - Men: `BMR = (10 × weight_kg) + (6.25 × height_cm) - (5 × age) + 5`
     - Women: `BMR = (10 × weight_kg) + (6.25 × height_cm) - (5 × age) - 161`
   - **Katch-McArdle Formula (For Athletes with known Body Fat %):**
     - `BMR = 370 + (21.6 × LBM_kg)` where `LBM = weight_kg × (1 - body_fat_pct / 100)`
2. **Physical Activity Level (PAL) Multipliers:**
   - Sedentary (office job, little/no exercise): `BMR × 1.2`
   - Lightly Active (light exercise 1–3 days/week): `BMR × 1.375`
   - Moderately Active (moderate training 3–5 days/week): `BMR × 1.55`
   - Very Active (hard exercise 6–7 days/week): `BMR × 1.725`
   - Extremely Active (endurance athlete / hard physical labor): `BMR × 1.9`
3. **The 4 Pillars of TDEE Decomposition:**
   - `TDEE = BMR (~60-70%) + NEAT (~15%) + TEF (~10%) + EAT (~5-10%)`
4. **The Unique Neuro-Metabolic Factor (Brand Connection):**
   - The human brain weighs only ~2% of total body mass, yet consumes **~20% of resting Basal Metabolic Energy (~300–450 kcal/day)** exclusively in glucose to sustain neuronal sodium-potassium electrochemical pumps. Visualizing this organ energy allocation makes our tool uniquely fascinating for FreeIQExam.com!
5. **Safety Guardrails & Clinical Caution (CRITICAL):**
   - **Starvation Floor Warning:** Display an explicit safety alert if a deficit drops below **1,200 kcal/day for women** or **1,500 kcal/day for men**, warning against metabolic slowdown, hormonal disruption (amenorrhea, low testosterone), and gallstone risks.
   - **Tiered Deficit Recommendations:**
     - *Maintenance:* Exactly 100% TDEE
     - *Mild Cut (-250 kcal/day):* ~0.25 kg / 0.5 lb loss per week (Preserves 100% lean muscle)
     - *Standard Moderate Cut (-500 kcal/day):* ~0.5 kg / 1.0 lb loss per week (The clinical gold standard)
     - *Aggressive Cut (-20% to -25% TDEE max):* For rapid intervention with high-protein caution.
     - *Lean Muscle Bulk (+250 to +350 kcal/day):* Clean surplus for hypertrophy.
